Shakrain Festival Memories

Orator: Mr. Farooq

Game: Shakrain / Kite Flying

Location: Old Dhaka

"In the past, preparations would begin months before the festival. We used to make our own kites and apply glass-coated starch (manja) to the strings ourselves. Now, most people buy ready-made kites. As a result, it can be said that Shakrain has now become a one-day festival."

"Back then, there weren't so many sources of entertainment. There were no mobile phones or the internet either. So, young and old alike waited eagerly for this day. It was a completely different feeling."

Past Memories of Traditional Games in Mymensingh

Recorded in Mymensingh • Field Oral History Archive

Orator: Rohul Amin

Location: Mymensingh, Bangladesh

Game: Dariabandha

"Back in the day, we used to play this a lot in Mymensingh. People our age would gather on open fields, draw lines on the ground, and play these games together. Sometimes, they were organized on a grand scale."

"The objective of capturing land by defeating opponents in this game is often seen as a reflection of feudal mindsets, where feudal lords established power by forcibly occupying others' territory. Experts also suggest that the game may have originated from real-life social experiences of salt theft and catching thieves—where the thief steals salt from the salt house (nun-ghor) to stack it in their hideout (gadi-ghor), while the guard attempts to thwart them."

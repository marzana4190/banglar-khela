Preserving Living Heritage

Banglar Khela is a community-driven digital humanities initiative dedicated to preserving traditional Bangladeshi folk games. Beyond recording static rules, our archive safeguards the living, embodied practices and regional oral knowledge that give these traditions their cultural life.

Why We Built This Website

With the rapid rise of digital games, open playground commercialization, and urban expansion, traditional native games are being systematically erased by globalized media.

While tangible artifacts like monuments and manuscripts are preserved in institutional archives, intangible folk games receive little digital preservation. Without action to record these living practices, indigenous traditions risk vanishing within three generations.

Our Aim & Objectives

We aim to safeguard intangible play heritage by anchoring local memory into sustainable digital storage:

01

Document Living Heritage

Record traditional games, regional variants, and oral rhymes directly from local practitioners.

02

Open Academic Repository

Provide a free, public archive for students, researchers, and cultural enthusiasts worldwide.

03

Digital Humanities Advocacy

Demonstrate how digital platforms can actively safeguard endangered indigenous knowledge.

What We Believe & Ethical Commitment

We believe native games are not mere pastimes—they are living expressions of community identity, bodily memory, and oral heritage.

"Grounded in Jan Assmann’s theory of Cultural Memory, we recognize that everyday folk practices require institutional digital forms to transition from temporary communicative memory into stable, long-term heritage."

Ethical Consent: We prioritize community partnership and informed consent. All oral histories, photographs, and audio recordings are collected respectfully, giving fair credit and protecting local communities from misrepresentation or commercial exploitation.
